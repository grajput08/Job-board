QA Job Finder NCR + Remote

Fresh QA / Testing / Automation / SDET roles — minimum experience under 3 years, posted within the last 7 days, in NCR or Remote-India.

Demo data. The built-in feed is synthetic sample data for showing how the board works — not real vacancies. Plug in a real feed via a JSON URL or a custom adapter (see js/adapters.js and the README).

Eligibility rules applied
  • Experience: minimum required experience must be < 3 years (no maximum limit).
  • Location: NCR (Noida, Greater Noida, Delhi, New Delhi, Gurgaon/Gurugram, Delhi NCR) or Remote-India (Remote, Remote India, WFH, Work From Home, Fully Remote, Pan India Remote, Anywhere in India).
  • Freshness: posted within the last 7 days.
  • Role: QA / Quality / Testing / Automation / SDET title.
  • Verifiable: a usable LinkedIn post URL must be present — unverifiable jobs are excluded, never guessed.
